BrainBloom Kids Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version: BrainBloom Kids, made by Say Studios LLC, does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

BrainBloom Kids saves game progress on the device it is played on: each child's nickname and age, chosen avatar, Pixel the robot's look and room, coins, stars, stickers, game levels, practice history and settings (such as sound, read-aloud, calm mode and the daily play-time limit). A family can add more than one child; each child's progress is kept separately on the same device.

This information never leaves the device. We have no servers, no accounts and no way to see it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login, email address or real names required. Children use a nickname.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that collects data.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • No in-app purchases, chat, public profiles, notifications or links to other websites.

Read-aloud

When read-aloud is on, instructions are spoken using the device's built-in voice. This happens on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Not a medical product

BrainBloom Kids is a set of thinking and puzzle games for fun and practice. It does not diagnose, treat or measure any condition.

Children's privacy

BrainBloom Kids is made for children and is designed to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by not collecting personal information at all.

Parents' controls

The Parents area is protected by a grown-up check. There a parent can see progress, add or switch children, set a daily play-time limit, change sound and read-aloud settings, and erase one child's data or everything. Deleting the app also deletes all of its saved information.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
